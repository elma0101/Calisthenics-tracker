# Forma skill catalog

Research and expansion: 30 September 2026. The inventory now contains **64 entries**, including **42 added entries**, with 255 trackable variations.

This is a broad curated collection of foundations, strength skills, balance, rings, mobility, and freestyle. Calisthenics has many combinations and new tricks, so there is no finite universally required list. Choose skills for your own goals; the Specialist branch is optional. Levels are editorial browsing labels for movement families, not individual readiness assessments or official competition grades.

## Added entries

- **Foundations:** Chin-up, Inverted row, Dead hang, Plank, Side plank, Bodyweight squat.
- **Balance:** Headstand, Forearm stand, Press to handstand, One-arm handstand, Handstand shoulder taps, Handstand walk.
- **Push:** One-arm push-up, Pseudo planche push-up, Planche push-up, 90-degree push-up, Russian dip, Straight-bar dip.
- **Pull:** Archer pull-up, Typewriter pull-up, Front lever pull-up, Hefesto.
- **Core:** V-sit, Manna, Hanging windshield wipers.
- **Legs:** Shrimp squat, Cossack squat, Nordic curl, Sissy squat, Dragon squat.
- **Rings:** False-grip hang, Ring handstand, Iron cross, Maltese.
- **Mobility:** Bridge, Pancake, Front split, Middle split.
- **Freestyle:** Bar swing, Bar pullover, Swing 180, Swing 360.

## Keeping records comparable

- Every variation has its own record. Assisted work and full skills stay separate.
- For one-sided movements, log separate efforts and identify the side in session notes unless the variation already specifies it.
- Handstand walking uses continuous seconds. Put distance or step count in notes.
- Typewriter pulls and hanging windshield wipers use a full return cycle as one rep; shoulder taps count each completed tap.
- Mobility entries record time, not measured flexibility. Note range and support height to keep comparisons useful.
- Freestyle entries count completed catches in the selected variation. Static illustrations show a labeled phase, not the entire movement.
- V-sit now has a dedicated entry. Existing L-sit / V-sit history and goals stay valid and are not moved or merged automatically.
- Images depict one labeled position or preparation. They are illustrations, not technique certification.
